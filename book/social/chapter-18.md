Chapter 18 of Learning Rust by Building FastMCP is out: transforms and visibility.

FastMCP prefixes names, renames tool arguments, marks components enabled or disabled, and can expose prompts and resources as tools. A visibility change sends a list-changed notification. The Rust chapter is a catalog, a `Cow` for the prefix, and a `list_changed` flag.

[personal note]

Written for a reader who is new to Rust. `Cow` borrows the name when the prefix is empty and owns it when there is a prefix to add.
